Keep an eye on your Claude Code and Codex quotas without clicking anything.

## What you get

- A usage card pinned above the sidebar footer icons, visible on every screen.
- One bar per quota window (5h session, weekly, model windows like Fable) showing the percentage left and a reset countdown.
- Colors that turn amber at 80% and red at 95%.
- A compact one-line-per-provider mode, and settings to hide providers or single gauges you don't care about.

## How it works

The card reads the same host-local usage BB's own Provider usage plugin shows, on the machine you pick: the primary one, the one running the open thread, or any other. It refreshes every couple of minutes, on focus, and right after a thread finishes a turn. Click the footer icon to hide or re-pin it.
